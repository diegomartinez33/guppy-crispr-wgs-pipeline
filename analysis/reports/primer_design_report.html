⚠ miosina_guppy_F/R: no identifiable binding site in any guppy genome

Exhaustive search: 0 exact matches across the whole v1 genome; 0 matches within 2 mismatches against the 58 genes of the myosin family in v1 (myh*/myo*/myl*, 156 transcripts, full spliced mRNA). Independently repeated against v2 (male, more complete annotation): 64 candidate genes, 199 transcripts — again 0 matches, even with 2 mismatches. Two independent assemblies and annotations agree on having no myosin-family gene compatible with these primers, which rules out incomplete annotation of a single genome as the explanation. This is not a population-variant issue — it suggests these primers were designed against a different species' sequence or a transcript model that never existed in P. reticulata. If RT-qPCR fails systematically with this pair, this is the most likely reason, independent of the samples' population of origin.

Methodology: localization via exact substring search (+ reverse complement) against the whole genome; for primers with no contiguous genomic match, spliced-mRNA reconstruction (exons concatenated per the annotation) to confirm they cross a real exon-exon junction and pinpoint the breakpoint; population verification via exact liftover (.chain) of each genomic segment to the Colombian pseudogenome and direct sequence comparison. Full session detail in CLAUDE.md ("RT-qPCR primer verification").

Methods (On-/Off-Target Primer Design)

eprimer3 (EMBOSS 6.6.0 + primer3_core 1.1.4) over a ±500bp window around the estimated cut site, excluding ±75bp immediately around the cut. Parameters: Tm 58-62°C (optimum 60), GC 40-60%, size 200-400bp, up to 5 candidates per site. Specificity checked with primersearch against the whole genome (10% mismatch tolerance) — SPECIFIC = a single amplicon; WARN_MULTI_HIT = the primer would also amplify other region(s) of the genome (positions listed). Population variants: exact liftover via a .chain file + minimap2 --cs against the Colombian pseudogenome, flagging whether any variant falls within the forward or reverse primer's genomic footprint.

⚠ off_target_1 and off_target_7 have no candidates: the reference window contains ambiguous IUPAC codes (K/M/R/S/W/Y — unresolved heterozygous sites in the 2014 assembly) that primer3_core rejects entirely. Confirmed this does not affect the already-reported GATK/CRISPResso2 results for these 2 sites (their actual analysis windows are much narrower and clean) — see docs/PIPELINE.md. Will be repeated against the v2 genome once available.
